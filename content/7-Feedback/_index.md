### Overall Evaluation

**1. Work Environment**

The work environment is very friendly and open. FCAJ members are always ready to help when I encounter difficulties, even outside of working hours. The workspace is neat and comfortable, which helps me focus better. However, I think there should be more team-building activities to help everyone understand each other better.

**2. Support from Mentors / Admin**

Mentors provide very detailed guidance, explain clearly when I don't understand, and always encourage me to ask questions. The admin team supports with procedures, documentation, and the necessary conditions to help me work effectively. I especially appreciate that mentors let me solve problems myself instead of just giving me the answers.

**3. Relevance of Work to Major**

The assigned tasks align well with what I have learned at university while also introducing me to new areas I hadn't explored before, especially cloud computing and serverless architecture. This helps me both strengthen my foundation and learn practical skills.

**4. Learning and Skill Development Opportunities**

During the internship, I learned many new skills such as designing serverless architectures, working with AWS Step Functions, writing infrastructure as code, and using CI/CD pipelines. Mentors also shared many practical experiences, which helped me better shape my career path.

**5. Company Culture and Team Spirit**

The company culture is very positive: everyone respects each other, works seriously, and maintains a friendly atmosphere. When there is an urgent project, everyone works together and supports each other regardless of their roles. This makes me feel like I am a part of the team, even as an intern.

**6. Internship Policies and Benefits**

The company provides flexible working hours when needed. Additionally, participating in internal training sessions and practical workshops is a significant advantage.

### Personal Reflections After the Internship

I feel that the program has a clear learning roadmap and dedicated support from the mentor team. There are many sharing sessions from Cloud Engineers and Solution Architects working in the industry, which provide a wealth of practical knowledge and experience for students.

After participating in the FCAJ Bootcamp, I have learned many valuable lessons — from technical knowledge to equally important soft skills, such as teamwork, progress management, searching for technical documentation, presenting ideas, and problem-solving. These experiences make me more confident as I prepare to enter the workforce after graduation.

I will recommend the FCAJ Bootcamp to students interested in Cloud Computing, DevOps, or AI, because FCAJ is a high-quality learning environment with practical content and active support from the community.

In the future, I hope to continue participating in FCAJ's in-depth programs on AWS Solution Architecture and DevOps on AWS to further enhance my knowledge and skills. I also hope to have the opportunity to contribute to the FCAJ community through academic activities and practical projects.

Finally, I would like to sincerely thank the mentors, the Admin team, and the organizing committee of the First Cloud AI Journey Bootcamp for always guiding, supporting, and accompanying me throughout my internship. This experience has helped me grow in terms of professional knowledge, work skills, and problem-solving mindset.

In particular, I would like to express my sincere gratitude to Mr. Nguyen Gia Hung — Head of Solutions Architecture / SA Director at AWS Vietnam — who built and led the FCAJ community, where I had the opportunity to learn, develop, and explore many new technologies. His guidance, practical experience, and support helped me clearly see the areas I need to improve, inspiring me to continue learning and perfecting myself every day.
